Room Types, Costs & Payment

Don’t miss a minute of campus life! Read more about each hall, including room types, special features and costs.

Which Housing Set-Up Is Right for You?

Choosing where you’ll live during your time at PNW is more than just picking a room, it’s about finding the environment that helps you thrive. Whether you’re looking for a lively, community-focused space or a quieter retreat, we offer options designed to fit different lifestyles and preferences.

Our multi-person suites are a great choice for students who thrive on collaboration and connection.

  • Shared Living Spaces: Your kitchen and common area are the perfect places to unwind with your friends and suitemates.
  • Private Bedrooms: Ideal for striking a balance between personal space and the energy of living alongside others.
  • Shared Bedrooms: Perfect for students wanting a roommate and suitemates at a lower price!

Our single and double residence hall rooms are a classic choice for those who prefer simplicity and convenience.

  • Shared Spaces: Designed for community, these areas make it easy to bond, study and relax together. Bathrooms and common areas will be shared between residents on your floor. A kitchen is available on the first floor.
  • Single Rooms: Ideal if you value privacy, quiet and a space that’s entirely your own.
  • Double Rooms: Great for building close friendships and sharing the college experience side by side.

No matter which setup you choose, you’ll enjoy the best of both worlds: a built-in community and a comfortable place to recharge, all within the larger campus family!

Now Open: Calumet Hall!

Purdue University Northwest’s newest residence hall is open and ready for the 2026-27 academic year!

The three-story residence hall provides 150 beds of single- and double-occupancy rooms, plus a common kitchen, laundry facilities, student study areas and a student lounge.

Room Types Available

You may express your preference for these spaces in your housing application, but if space is limited, you will be placed in any room type below. Room assignments are first-come, first-served, so please be sure to complete all of your application items as quickly as possible and by the priority application date!
