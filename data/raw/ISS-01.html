We support internationalization and global awareness through on-campus programming as well as welcoming international students.

Health Insurance

All international undergraduate and graduate students who are registered, regardless of credit hours, or in an approved professional certificate program must enroll in the mandatory university health insurance plan unless you meet the criteria for a waiver.

Supporting International Students and Scholars

Committed to the internationalization of Purdue University Northwest, we provide services to international clientele as well as  university and offices. We also enhance the academic, cultural and social pursuits of students and scholars from abroad through expertise in immigration, advising, and cross-cultural programming.

Immigration Services

Immigration information is the most important service we offer. We are the expert resource in the areas of F-1 and J-1 rules and regulations.We have information regarding the new administration’s changes to immigration law or practice in the U.S.,  including travel, visa issuance, Executive Orders and special FAQs.

  • Guidance on maintaining good immigration Status
  • Extension-of-stay applications; transfer to or from other U.S. institution
  • Employment applications, including on- and off-campus options
  • Reinstatement applications
  • Travel endorsements
  • Reduced course load Authorization
  • Applications to change non-immigrant status
  • Production of immigration documents for prospective undergraduate and graduate students
  • Exchange Visitor Program (J-1 student and scholar visa information)
