Housing

College is a time of growth, inside and outside of the classroom. In PNW housing, you can fully immerse yourself in the experience with comfortable, convenient housing while learning and making lifelong friends!

Welcome to Your New Home on Campus

Get ready for an exciting college experience on our vibrant Hammond campus, perfect for you and your new best friends!

We offer traditional and suite-style rooms in our three residence halls.

MOVE-IN DATES

  • Furnished rooms: Comfortable living with all the essentials for just you or shared with a roommate.
  • Laundry facilities: Easy access to laundry right where you need it.
  • 24-hour service desks and staff on call: Assistance is always available, day or night, for any need or emergency.
  • Versatile study spaces: Use study rooms for focused work and group sessions.
  • Outdoor patios: Relax with friends or enjoy yard games
  • WiFi and printers throughout the complex: Stay connected and print your work wherever you are.
  • Exciting events year-round: From movie nights and game nights to holiday celebrations, PNW residence halls bring the fun to you!
  • Walkable campus: Less than a mile from every campus building.
  • Right by the fitness center: Stay active and energized.

Room Types and Rates

Purdue University Northwest offers a variety of residential living options, with six distinct room types for students to choose from. Whether you’re looking for a cozier space or a shared environment with friends, there’s something to fit every preference and lifestyle. Find the perfect room to make your college experience comfortable and personalized!

Explore detailed room types and costs

The prices listed below reflect the full academic year costs for 2026-27. Prices may change. Please visit our cost and payment page for pricing, available room types and payment information.

Single Room

$8,000 – 2026-27 Rate

For first-year students!

  • Bedroom: Private
  • Bathroom: Private (Shared with Floor)
  • Kitchen and Common Area: Communal with Building
  • Location: Calumet Hall

Double Room

$6,250 – 2026-27 Rate

For first-year students!

  • Bedroom: Shared with One Student
  • Bathroom: Private (Shared with Floor)
  • Kitchen and Common Area: Communal with Building
  • Location: Calumet Hall

Two-Person Suite

$8,760 – 2026-27 Rate

  • Bedroom: Private
  • Bathroom: Shared with One Student
  • Kitchen and Common Area: Shared with One Student
  • Location: Griffin Hall

Two-person suites are offered only in Griffin Hall. If unavailable, you’ll be placed in a four-bedroom suite.

  • Kitchen and Common Area: Shared with Four Students
  • Location: Griffin and Peregrine Halls
