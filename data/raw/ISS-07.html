New Student Orientation

Get ready to be part of PNW! New Student Orientation (NSO) is a chance to have fun and get everything you need to feel welcome and confident as you begin your Purdue University Northwest journey.

What to Expect

Led by PNW Campus Ambassadors, your first day offers opportunities to meet classmates, engage with PNW staff and faculty, access campus resources and understand college expectations—all while getting to know PNW. While attending NSO, students will receive their PNW student ID, PNW gear, a PNW BOUND yard sign and meet new friends in their classes.
